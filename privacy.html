Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Operator

This Privacy Policy applies to the Seron service operated as a sole proprietorship under the trade name 'Seron'. Contact: seroninjapan@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information: (1) LINE profile information (display name, LINE user ID); (2) area of residence (prefecture/city); (3) nationality; (4) survey responses and feedback submitted after experiences; (5) participation history (experiences applied for and attended).

3. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used for: (1) matching members with suitable experience opportunities; (2) communicating via LINE regarding monitor opportunities; (3) providing anonymized survey feedback to partner businesses; (4) improving the Seron service.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Survey feedback shared with partner businesses is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individual members.

5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for the duration of membership. Upon withdrawal (blocking the LINE account), personal data will be deleted within 30 days upon request.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Please contact us at seroninjapan@gmail.com.

7. Compliance

Seron handles personal information in compliance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
