About Seron

What is Seron?

A monitor service for foreigners living in Japan

Seron is a community platform that connects foreigners living in Japan. Share your honest opinions about Japanese food, culture, services, and society — your feedback directly reaches Japanese companies and policymakers.

Why Join?

Free experiences, real rewards

🎉 100% Free

Experience costs covered

Restaurant meals, beauty treatments, and cultural activities are all provided at no cost to you.

📝 No SNS Posting

Just fill out a survey

No need to post on Instagram or any social media. All we ask is an honest survey response after your experience.
